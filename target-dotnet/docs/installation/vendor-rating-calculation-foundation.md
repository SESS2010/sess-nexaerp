# Vendor-rating calculation foundation

The calculation foundation retains evidence identities and revisions for quality and delivery; accepts only explicit governed warranty, payment-term and required-document rules; and preserves typed historical scores as LEGACY. Original ERP version zero is valid evidence. Missing identities, negative revisions or missing source facts cannot become full marks.

Quality is calculated per received line with concession quantities retained separately. Delivery uses the specified 20-point lateness scale. The other three automatic dimensions have no invented default scale. Technical, Response and Overall remain manual; a combined GRN score must not multiply shipment/manual marks by line count. Mixed-unit quality aggregation and receipt weighting require the approved policy.

LEGACY records retain source hash/sheet/row, bill and vendor identity, original dimensions and original total, including total discrepancies. They cannot be presented as measured. No 1,057-bill or 225-vendor import is claimed without the actual workbook.

This package adds current quality/delivery source evidence and governed manual QC Manager assessments; see vendor-rating-source-evidence.md and vendor-manual-assessment.md. Remaining automatic source rules, governed rule administration, complete receipt history/rolling ratings, PO blocking and historical import remain outstanding. The legacy archive identifies GRN `receive.warranty` as free text and vendor-rating `warrantyMonths` as its old derived field. Actual records and a validated structured-month mapping into the current GRN are still needed, together with approved scoring/weighting definitions. The old archive formula has different weights and cannot authorize the new scoring rules. The current GRN expiry generated as bill date plus thirteen months is not supplier warranty evidence. Delivery-date evidence exists in the PO-linked approved comparison line's retained DeliverySnapshot; no additional date field should be invented merely to duplicate it.
