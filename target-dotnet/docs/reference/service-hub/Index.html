🗄 Year-End Archive

Step 1 Create archive copies a financial year to Google Drive (Google Sheet + Excel file you can download to PC). Nothing is removed.
Step 2 Remove from app (only after 31 March) deletes from the app only the rows that are inside that archive. A full safety copy of the whole sheet is saved first.
Open complaints / tasks are never archived or removed – they stay with their full history. Customers, machines, employees and settings always stay.
