Important distinctions:

The rule version is `SESS-EIGHT-DIMENSIONS-20260919-V1`. These live in [rating measurements][ratingMeasured] and [score validation][ratingLegacy], not a settings editor. Historical marks remain marked LEGACY, not automatic measurements.

- Old `Rev869BSeedData`/workbook seeds include 50,000 / 5,00,000-era bands. `ApprovalConfigurationAndPermissionsPart2Sql` supersedes them with the current department-first 5,000 / 1,00,000 rules. Do not build a screen against historical constants.
- PR uses estimated value; PO/comparison uses payable value. The selector accepts a decimal and has no currency argument/FX conversion; its caller passes the document total. This report therefore does **not** certify these numeric purchase limits as INR-equivalent for every foreign-currency document. Define/witness that basis before such use; a future page must display it and preserve conversion evidence.
- Effective purchase steps, snapshots, tax/QC decisions and append-only Stores versions are useful foundations, not a complete maker-checker settings facility. Stores versions contain changer/reason/time but no separate checker; their current guard recognises TD, MD and IT Manager. The proposed business page must not silently inherit IT Manager control-change authority.

## Proposed Business Configuration page — after 1 October

A TD/MD page with **Current values**, **Proposed changes**, **Awaiting another person's decision**, **Scheduled versions** and **History**. Link existing domain workflows instead of duplicating QC/tax decision engines.

The shared **6–10 developer-day foundation** includes:

1. Typed, allowlisted settings with company scope, units, bounds, dependencies, business owner and a named consuming operation. No arbitrary JSON keys, SQL, environment variables, filesystem paths or scripts.
2. Immutable proposals with before/after values, reason, maker's resolved EmployeeId, company, expected current version and effective date. Draft editing is not activation.
3. **A different human checker**, enforced by EmployeeId across accounts/realms, with current company authority and MFA. TD proposes/MD checks or MD proposes/TD checks under the approved authority model; existing domain requirements such as CFO period authority remain additional constraints. No self-approval by switching roles/accounts.
4. Control changes approved under the **previously active** policy, not a proposed weaker rule. Changing the checker/approval policy itself needs independent approval. If no eligible independent checker exists, refuse activation; no self-approval fallback.
5. Immutable effective versions, non-overlapping coverage and atomic activation for interdependent thresholds/steps/routes. Reject gaps, overlaps, missing actors, invalid units and ambiguous boundaries. Avoid retrospective activation into used periods. Cancellation/rollback creates a new event/version, not an edit to history.
6. Explicit selection time per workflow: submission/approval-cycle creation, receipt date or tax point as appropriate. Retain rule ID/version, evaluated amount/basis and resolved authority. Existing documents keep their approval rule; revision creates a new documented cycle. Historical snapshots never preserve a revoked person's present access rights.
7. Transactional audit of maker/checker, timestamps, before/after values, reason, effective range, rejection/cancellation and activation result. Concurrency/idempotency prevent lost updates and duplicate decisions; runtime principals must not bypass approval with direct table writes. Apply this to every existing mutation path, including quick-add, reactivation, imports and direct configuration APIs, not just the new page.
8. Boundary, self-approval, cross-company, stale-version, replay, future-activation and historical-document tests. A hidden button is not enforcement.

First post-login group: foundation, purchase/A2/item-age controls, and links to existing tax/QC workflows. Broader master governance, optional expense/email/rating and report presentation follow separately. The whole inventory is not a small single-page task; approval, history and consumers are the larger part.
